# Independent Challenge Review: LG65NANO756PR Native Bootable OS Plan

## 1. Executive Verdict & Phase E Separability

* **Plan Status:** **NOT READY FOR UNMODIFIED APPROVAL** (even for Phase E). While the plan rightly refuses to fabricate boot recipes or bypasses, it contains **contradictory agent execution directives**, **unresolvable binary-supply dependencies**, **impossible milestone sequencing (M2/M3/M4)**, and **hidden scope drift** between partial-stack replacement and stock webOS preservation.
* **Is Phase E (Evidence-Only Approval) Separable from Future Deployment?**
  * **Yes, in principle:** Box-only inspection of public LG OSS archives (`sequence 510320`), passive normal-UI observation (`U1`), and documentary checks for boot/recovery routes (`U6`/`U7`) incur zero device risk and do not commit the owner to Phase B (build) or Phase T (target deployment).
  * **Not as currently drafted:** Phase E is currently entangled with three defects that must be edited out first:
    1. The plan header commands agentic workers (`REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: ... executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task`) to execute checkboxes across the entire document, contradicting `PLAN ONLY` and phase boundaries.
    2. `Approval scopes` states that archive-inspection tooling (`Task 2`) requires a *separate* "executable test-first tooling addendum and review before running on untrusted archives," meaning Phase E cannot execute `Task 2` steps 3–5 or `Task 3` until that tooling addendum exists.
    3. Phase E omits the documentary check for `U6`/`U7` (`Task 4`), deferring the highest-probability existential stop condition (lack of a non-bypass retail custom-boot/recovery route) behind exhaustive archive parsing (`Task 2`/`Task 3`).

## 2. Architecture Boundary Challenge: A vs. B vs. C

The plan distinguishes Architectures A, B, and C at a high level, but masks critical dependency traps unique to each route:

| Dimension | Arch A: Retained Vendor Kernel + BSP / New Userspace (Partial-Stack) | Arch B: Rebuilt Kernel + Vendor BSP / New Userspace (Kernel Replacement) | Arch C: Independent Kernel / Full Hardware Port (Full Port) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Actual Replacement Boundary** | Retains exact signed vendor kernel binary, kernel modules (`kdriver`), and proprietary hardware blobs; replaces `init`, rootfs, and UI. | Compiles kernel/GPL modules from OSS archive (`4.4.84-711.kalaupapa.4` lead); links against vendor BSP blobs; replaces rootfs/UI. | Clean-room kernel + drivers for SoC, T-CON/panel, HDMI-RX, HDCP, audio, and PMIC/Micom. |
| **Unacknowledged Binary-Supply Blocker** | **Where do retained binaries come from?** Public OSS archives (`S3`) distribute GPL/LGPL *source*, not the compiled stock binary kernel, proprietary modules, or rootfs blobs. Without an authorized way to read/extract the TV's internal flash or decrypt retail firmware updates, Arch A has no lawful supply of the "retained" binaries (`Task 3`). | Same binary-supply blocker for non-GPL BSP blobs (`packagegroup-starfish-bsp`, panel LUTs, HDMI/HDCP firmware, `omx-il-rtk`), plus OSS archives often omit closed-source build headers/generators (`U4`). | None (relies on public hardware datasheets), but blocked because public SoC/TV-pipeline documentation is non-existent (`U5`). |
| **Userspace-to-Hardware Coupling Trap** | Linux kernel (`kdriver`) on webOS TVs rarely exposes standard DRM/KMS, V4L2, or ALSA interfaces for HDMI-RX, HDCP, and panel dimming; hardware initialization is driven by proprietary userspace daemons (`videooutputd`, `audiooutputd`). Replacing userspace breaks hardware unless those daemons—and their IPC/Luna dependencies—are also retained. | Rebuilding the GPL kernel does not change the `kdriver` ABI: the new userspace still faces custom vendor ioctls or requires retaining proprietary userspace daemons that may refuse to run on a modified kernel/libc ABI. | Requires clean-room driver reimplementation; impossible without register-level documentation and licensed HDCP sink integration. |
| **Boot & Cryptographic Binding** | Even if the kernel binary is untouched, retail secure boot typically binds `initramfs`/rootfs integrity (`dm-verity` or signed rootfs hash) to the signed kernel image. | Requires a legitimate retail mechanism to accept a custom-signed or unsigned kernel image (`U6`), plus kernel module signature compatibility with proprietary `.ko` binaries. | Same boot authorization barrier (`U6`) as Arch B. |

## 3. Unsafe Implicit Assumptions, Missing Dependencies, Scope Drift & Acceptance Gaps

### A. Unsafe Implicit Assumptions
1. **Non-Invasive Hardware Identity Resolution (`U1` + `U2` / `Task 1`):**
   * *Assumption:* Normal UI (`TV Information`) plus public parts catalogs (`S4`/`S5`) will deterministically identify the installed PCB (`EBU66527491` vs. `EAX69581701`), panel variant (`BOE` vs. others), and SoC without opening the chassis.
   * *Flaw:* Normal UI menus rarely expose mainboard PCB revisions, panel BOM variants, or SoC steppings, and a single retail suffix (e.g., `.AEU` or `.AEK`) routinely spans multiple interchangeable factory mainboard/panel assemblies.
2. **OSS Archive Sufficiency (`U3` / `Task 3`):**
   * *Assumption:* Inspecting the 3 OSS archives (`sequence 510320`) can prove hardware coverage (`U5`) and ABI compatibility (`Task 3`).
   * *Flaw:* GPL/LGPL compliance tarballs only contain open-source components. They will not contain proprietary userspace binaries (`videooutputd`, `audiooutputd`), TEE/HDCP trusted applications, or factory calibration partitions.
3. **Licensed HDCP & Calibration Survival (`Goal`, `M3`, `V04`):**
   * *Assumption:* A replacement userspace (Arch A) or rebuilt kernel (Arch B) can negotiate licensed HDCP 1.4/2.2 as an HDMI sink and apply factory panel calibration without copying credentials or bypassing DRM.
   * *Flaw:* HDMI sink HDCP authentication relies on encrypted keys stored in locked NVRAM/RPMB accessed via a Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) and signed vendor middleware. Replacing userspace or the kernel typically severs the authenticated TEE client path.

### B. Missing Dependencies & Contradictory Milestone Sequencing
1. **The "Blind & Mute Boot" Contradiction in `M2` vs. `M3`/`M4`:**
   * `M2 (smallest boot)` requires booting minimal `init` and capturing health/liveness logs through an "authorized interface" *before* `M3 (panel/backlight)` and `M4 (network)`.
   * Yet `Safety invariants` forbid "chassis opening, serial console wiring." Without panel output (`M3`), network (`M4`), or UART/serial console (prohibited), **there is no authorized interface to observe `M2` logs or liveness**.
2. **Power/Thermal/Watchdog Sequencing Hazard (`M2` before `M3`/`M5`):**
   * Booting a minimal `init` in `M2` before panel power-sequencing (`M3`) and PMIC/Micom thermal/watchdog management (`M5`) risks hardware watchdog resets, unsequenced T-CON/backlight power states, or loss of physical remote/button standby control.
3. **Target-Specific vs. Generic Recovery Validation (`Task 4` / `U7`):**
   * `Task 4` allows validating recovery on "an exact compatible development target." However, proving recovery on a second board does *not* prove that the primary unit's **unique per-unit calibration and HDCP provisioning data** can be backed up non-invasively before the first write on the primary unit.

### C. Scope Drift & Acceptance Gaps
1. **Unbounded "Retained Vendor Binary" Drift (`Task 3` vs. `Goal`):**
   * `Task 3` permits retaining vendor binaries (`For each retained vendor binary record permitted use/distribution...`). If HDMI-IN, HDCP, and panel control require retaining `videooutputd`, `audiooutputd`, `starfish-bsp`, and their IPC stack, Arch A silently drifts into **"stripped stock webOS with a replacement UI"**—violating the core boundary (`not merely an app... or replacement launcher on otherwise-stock webOS`).
2. **UI Sliders Mistaken for Hardware Baseline (`Task 1`, `V02`, `V03`):**
   * Recording webOS UI numbers (`panel 40, contrast 80, brightness 50...`) in `validation/baseline.md` provides zero hardware-level acceptance criteria (PWM limits, T-CON register timings, voltage rails, or thermal thresholds) for `V03 (panel safety)`.
3. **Undefined Quantitative Acceptance in `V05` & `V09`:**
   * `V05 (audio)` defers lip-sync tolerance ("specified from baseline before target comparison"), and `V09 (sustained operation)` relies on thermal limits that may not be exposed in OSS sources (`if sensors/limits absent, block`).

## 4. Prioritized Blockers

1. **[P0 — Existential] No Documented Retail Custom-Boot or Independent Recovery Route (`U6`, `U7`, `Task 4`):** Confirmed "None verified." Neither `S6` (Dev Mode) nor `S7` (webOS OSE) provides boot chain unlock or independent flash recovery on retail `65NANO756PR`.
2. **[P0 — Existential] Missing Lawful Supply of Retained Binaries & Provisioning Backup (`Task 3`, `Task 4`):** Public OSS archives (`S3`) do not supply compiled proprietary BSP blobs, running kernel binaries, or unit-specific calibration/HDCP provisioning backups, and reading internal flash via exploit/hardware tap is prohibited.
3. **[P1 — Architectural] Unresolved Board/SoC/Panel Mapping Without Disassembly (`U1`, `U2`, `Task 1`):** Normal UI + suffix cannot guarantee disambiguation between candidate boards (`EBU66527491` vs. `EAX69581701`) and panel variants.
4. **[P1 — Safety/Sequencing] `M2` Liveness/Logging & Power/Watchdog Sequencing Contradiction:** Cannot observe `M2` without display (`M3`), network (`M4`), or forbidden serial console; cannot safely boot `M2` without Micom/watchdog/panel power-rail control.
5. **[P2 — Process/Governance] Agent Auto-Execution Header & Missing Tooling Addendum Gate in Phase E:** Plan header instructs agent sub-skills to execute tasks immediately, while `Task 2` lacks its required pre-execution tooling addendum.

## 5. Exact Plan Edits Required

Apply these specific edits to the `PLAN` before granting Phase E approval:

1. **Remove Agent Execution Header (Top of `PLAN`):**
   * *Delete:* `> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (- [ ]) syntax for tracking.`
   * *Replace with:* `> **PLANNING CONTRACT ONLY:** Do not invoke execution sub-skills or execute tasks. Every phase requires a separate, explicitly approved executable addendum.`
2. **Split Phase E into `Phase E1 (Read-Only Acquisition & Documentary Gate)` and `Phase E2 (Tooling Addendum & Archive Audit)` in `Approval scopes`:**
   * *Edit `Approval scopes`:* Define **Phase E1** as: (a) `Task 1` normal-UI read-only observation (`U1`), (b) `Task 2` steps 1–2 only (browser metadata capture + archive download + SHA256 verification, zero extraction/parsing), (c) `Task 4` step 1 documentary search for published manufacturer custom-boot/recovery documentation (`U6`/`U7`), and (d) drafting the test-first archive-reader tooling addendum. Define **Phase E2** as running the approved archive reader (`Task 2` steps 3–5) and `Task 3` source audit *only after* Phase E1 artifacts and tooling tests are reviewed.
3. **Add Binary-Supply & Middleware Ceiling Criteria to `U4`, `U5`, and `Task 3`:**
   * *Add to `Task 3` Acceptance:* `"Explicitly record where any required binary blob (kernel, kdriver module, firmware, or userspace daemon) is lawfully obtained if absent from the public OSS source archives. Define a strict middleware boundary: if hardware operation requires retaining stock webOS userspace daemons (e.g., videooutputd/audiooutputd/Luna bus), classify this as a blocked dependency rather than Architecture A compliance."`
4. **Fix `U2` / `Task 1` Stop Condition for Non-Invasive Identity:**
   * *Add to `Task 1` step 5:* `"If normal UI (U1) + authoritative manufacturer documentation cannot uniquely resolve the installed PCB revision, panel variant, and SoC without opening the chassis, halt at G1 and report U2 as permanently blocked under non-invasive constraints."`
5. **Fix `M2`–`M5` Safety & Observability Dependencies in `Conditional future milestones`:**
   * *Edit `M2`:* Require an explicitly identified, non-invasive authorized telemetry/log channel *and* verified PMIC/Micom watchdog and safe panel power-off/inhibit state as entry preconditions for `M2`, rather than deferring power/thermal safety to `M5` and display to `M3`.
6. **Require Primary-Unit Non-Invasive Backup Proof in `Task 4` (`U7`):**
   * *Edit `Task 4` step 5:* Specify that recovery validation on a secondary development board does not waive proving a non-invasive, authorized readout and restore path for the *primary unit's* unique calibration/provisioning data prior to any Phase T write.
