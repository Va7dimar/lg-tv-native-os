### 3. Command/Control Defects vs. Nonblocking Residuals
* **Command/Control Defects (Read-Only Research):** **None blocking.** The literal `isolate_inventory.py` invocation uses unchanged pinned tooling, RO-staged prior-pinned inputs, a fresh output path, exact 3-path canonical selection (`linux-4.4.3/README_HowToMake.txt`, `bootcode/README_Make_Boot.txt`, `bootcode/README-bootcode.txt`), `--max-selected-text-member-bytes 1217` (bounding the 3 guides to 3,442B), and shared ceilings (`200000` members, `100000000` pathname bytes, `4GiB` expanded, `10GiB` free disk floor, `120s` wall / `1GiB` AS).
* **Nonblocking Residuals (Read-Only Extraction):**
  * `--hash-input` computes/records input SHA256s without in-tool pin rejection, and no separate total-selected-bytes CLI flag exists. Bounded parsing without built-in pin rejection is acceptable here because inputs are prior-pinned regular files, canonical 3-path selection plus the `1217B` cap bounds output to `3442B`, and Gate 4 enforces post-run pin equality before trusting any output.
  * Observed (non-reserved) disk floor, AS/wall PAX backstops, and `0644` permissions (not `noexec`) are acceptable because extracted READMEs remain inert data.
* **Read-Only Research vs. Boot Implementation:** These controls suffice for host-side text extraction, whereas **in-TV boot implementation** remains blocked by unknown physical hardware/SoC, unread `$(PRJ).inc`, unknown boot chain/signing/recovery, and zero authorization to build or touch the TV.

* **Accept the proposed read-only contract and literal command** for parent execution handoff, explicitly accepting bounded `--hash-input` parsing subject to all pre-run checks (tool SHA256s, non-existent/non-symlink output path, input sizes, free disk) and Gate 4 validation (both input SHA256 pins matched, `OK` statuses, 64,174 aggregate members, exact 3 paths totaling 3,442B) prior to reading extracted text.
